The National Academy of Sciences and the Institute of Medicine state that
biological evolution — descent of species with modification through natural
processes including natural selection — is the central organizing principle
of modern biology and is supported by an overwhelming body of evidence drawn
from genetics, paleontology, comparative anatomy, biogeography, and direct
observation of evolutionary change in living populations.

The same publication notes that intelligent design and other forms of
creationism are not science, because they invoke supernatural causation
which cannot be tested empirically, and that they should not be taught in
public-school science classes. The American Association for the Advancement
of Science, the National Science Teaching Association, and the National
Center for Science Education hold equivalent positions.

This is the published institutional position of the cited bodies; this
entry does not address theological views, which are outside the scope of
empirical science.
