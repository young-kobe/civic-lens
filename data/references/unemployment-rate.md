The official measurement of U.S. unemployment is published monthly by the
Bureau of Labor Statistics in the Employment Situation release, derived
from the Current Population Survey (CPS, ~60,000 households per month) and
the Current Employment Statistics survey (~119,000 establishments).

BLS publishes six headline measures, U-1 through U-6. The "official"
unemployment rate cited in mainstream coverage is U-3: people without a
job who are available for work and have actively looked in the prior four
weeks, as a percentage of the civilian labor force. U-6, broader, also
counts marginally attached workers and those employed part-time for
economic reasons.

The current month's value, the prior month's revision, and the prior year's
comparison are all available on the BLS site at the URL above. Use that
release as the authoritative number rather than secondary citations, since
revisions are common in the month after first publication.
