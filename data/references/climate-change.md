The IPCC's Sixth Assessment Report (Synthesis Report, 2023) states that it
is unequivocal that human activities, principally through emissions of
greenhouse gases, have warmed the climate. Global surface temperature in
the most recent decade analyzed (2011-2020) was approximately 1.1 °C
higher than the 1850-1900 baseline.

The same report finds that observed increases in well-mixed greenhouse-gas
concentrations since around 1750 are unequivocally caused by human
activities, and that human-caused climate change is already affecting many
weather and climate extremes globally.

This is the position of the IPCC, an intergovernmental scientific body
whose assessment reports are produced by hundreds of authors and approved
line-by-line by member governments. Equivalent positions have been
published by the U.S. National Academies of Sciences, NASA, NOAA, and the
American Meteorological Society.
