# Phase 1. Claims and evidence

## Two opening reader questions

**What did House roll call 325 decide?** In release 1416 the vote page identified suspension procedure, a two-thirds threshold, and the tally. The purpose of H.R. 9600 was to approve a location for a Thomas Paine memorial. Reps. Raskin and Spartz discussed the measure in a release on November 17. Join the applicable bill text, House decision and dated statement. Attribute each quote to its speaker. Distinguish House approval from enactment and a completed memorial. The external statement is a candidate source. The [published sample](experiments/published-graph.md) found no published relationship records. [Bill](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/BILLS-119hr9600rh), [roll call](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2026325?Page=2) and [sponsors' statement](https://raskin.house.gov/2026/11/raskin-spartz-legislation-finalizing-details-for-national-memorial-honoring-indispensable-american-patriot-thomas-paine-passes-house).

**Which veterans-benefits claims can a reader check?** The proclamation mentions a fiscal count, a decline, an executive action and a housing goal. Its gratitude to veterans also supplies a useful example of a passage to skip. The two pilot executive records exposed metadata sources in our sample. Capture and validate the official body before extracting a publishable claim. Use R-61 for executive source coverage. [Proclamation](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-16/pdf/2026-23348.pdf) and [executive action pipeline](../architecture/executive-actions.md).

|---|---|---|
| VA processed a record 3.1 million disability claims this year | Fiscal period, population and comparable historical counts. VA reports 3,111,399 disability compensation claims in FY 2026. | Agreement with an attributed agency report. Independent confirmation needs a separate source lineage. | The administration aims to house up to 6,000 homeless veterans by 2028 | Preserve the qualifier, target population, due date and unit. Look for progress reports. | A commitment with a deadline. People housed, available beds and appropriated funds measure different things. |

The House passed the Major Richard Star Act on November 12. The proclamation was signed on November 10. The vote fits the issue timeline. Its existence cannot verify an outcome that the president described as already achieved.

## Proposed workflow and ownership

1. The collector fetches bounded official material under existing source rules. Separate campaign remarks, official actions, agency measurements and quoted third parties by origin.
2. Application proposes atomic claims from the saved body. Classify factual actions, numbers or comparisons, outcome or causal claims, positions and future commitments. Preserve the original passage, speaker, qualifiers, units, population and time window. Split a statement that claims both an action and its effect.
3. Retrieve candidates using explicit identifiers, official names, dates and program text. Seek contrary evidence and independent source families. An LLM may propose questions and supporting passages. Code validates spans and computes defined quantities. A missing body, ambiguous speaker or missing baseline becomes a visible gap.
4. Use the existing review queue and exact-version decision pattern. A reviewer decides disputed meaning, attribution and the interpretation of outcomes. Publication includes only eligible reviewed revisions and cited evidence. The public API exposes published contracts. [Relationship drafting and review](../architecture/statements.md#relationship-drafting), [analysis](../architecture/analysis.md) and [publication](../architecture/publication.md).

## Proposed relational design

| Unit | Identity and meaning |
|---|---|
| Claim and claim revision | Stable claim identity and an immutable revision. Bind the source record kind, record revision, exact extraction and span. Store the normalized proposition separately from the quote. Structured values can be unknown. |
| Evidence link | A directed relation between a claim revision and a typed evidence version. Cite a span, official fact or a dataset cell with its source digest. Keep the source family, event date, observation date, and evidence availability date when known. |
| Assessment revision | Bind one claim revision and the exact selected links. Record question, as-of cutoff, method version, calculation, contrary evidence, reviewer and limitations. A new finding adds a revision. |
| Published insight | Select the eligible assessment and evidence revisions in one release. If a dependency changes, flag the finding for review. A withdrawal invalidates affected public routes, features and caches. Retain correction history. |

These are responsibilities for schema discussion. Use typed foreign keys and existing revision owners where possible. Decide how to bind a spreadsheet region and how claim identity survives splits or merges before migration. Queue work with its trigger in the same transaction. Replaying the same extraction and task version should preserve identity. Exercise duplicate imports, stale review, withdrawal, and a failed run that retries. The approval of an exact quote establishes what was said. An assessment of its accuracy needs its own evidence.

The first assessment labels to test with reviewers are supported for the specified scope, conflicts with the specified evidence, mixed, insufficient evidence, and target pending. Collect human disagreement before choosing public wording. AVeriTeC offers a model for checking an answer through intermediate evidence questions and temporal cutoffs. [AVeriTeC study](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/cd86a30526cd1aff61d6f89f107634e4-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html).

## How to add an issue

The tracked issue is a way to find evidence across sources. Use a second, finer unit for the comparison: a policy mechanism affecting a defined population. For example, cap patient cost sharing for Medicare insulin and reduce insulin list prices for all buyers are different propositions. A spending bill can change health, energy and taxes in separate sections. A single provision can have several issue labels.

1. **Configure the issue.** Reuse the versioned catalog's stable ID, definition, effective dates where available, mapped policy areas, inclusion and exclusion examples. Add examples of neighboring issues that are easy to confuse. Allow an empty label set. Treat repeated unmapped passages as candidates for a new curated issue. [Catalog](../../config/issues.json) and [existing bill issue selection](../../src/CivicLens.Application/Issues/BillIssueChoice.cs).
2. **Find eligible passages.** Segment the bill edition into provisions, preserving subsection boundaries and exact spans. Save the claim span from the statement or order separately. Use official identifiers first, then lexical and semantic retrieval across issues. A bill's primary policy area is a useful hint. A secondary provision of an omnibus bill can fall outside the candidate set supplied by that area today. Measure those misses under R-54 before changing the gate.
3. **Propose a relationship.** An AI classifier can propose zero or more issue IDs for each passage, plus the actor, action, target population, mechanism, amount and period where stated. Keep the model run and exact citations. Compare each proposed pair at that level. A broad policy goal can match a subject while leaving the speaker's view of the specific provision unknown.
4. **Attach the official action.** Traverse from the member's dated cast to the roll-call question and its target bill, amendment or motion. Bind the version before the chamber and the status of the provision at that point. An amendment directly deciding one provision supplies more specific evidence than an omnibus final vote. A motion to table or waive a rule needs its own interpretation. Record absence, missing text and lack of an individual roll call explicitly.
5. **Choose the public wording.** Distinguish *discussed the same issue*, *addressed the same proposal*, *voted on the package*, *voted on the specific motion*, and *later outcome observed*. Interpreted support or conflict needs a reviewed match in scope and direction. A direct vote can verify a claim about that person's vote. An outcome claim needs implementation or measurement evidence. These are proposed evidence roles for R-48 and R-64. Make the schema decision after the trial.

Compare three methods on the same independently labeled spans: the existing policy-area candidate gate, lexical or embedding retrieval over all configured issues, and a multi-label model reading the definition and provision together. Score missed secondary issues, false topical links and unsupported claims about members separately. At low label volumes, try a model that reads the definitions without training. Compare a trained classifier only when each issue has enough independent positive and negative examples. Keep a change in the catalog version distinct from a change in model version.

The [cross-issue replay](experiments/issue-mapping.md) supplies development cases for these decisions. For the next trial, freeze a separate set of statement and provision spans before tuning a model. Include single-issue bills, omnibus provisions, competing bills on the same issue, amended text, voice votes and untracked topics. Reserve complete bill and statement families. Reserve an additional issue family that has familiar evidence types, then try onboarding it with catalog examples only. Track per-issue quality, review time and which additions needed engineering. A new issue on an existing source should usually need labels and examples. A new evidence type, such as agency outcome data, may need a new collector or verifier.

Test the same fields on an official statement, an executive order, and a campaign remark with a verified speaker. Add other members' responses by their own quotes. An office or party affiliation can define the cohort. It cannot supply a position for members who have not spoken. Finance, judicial and agency sources can later fill the implementation and outcome parts of the same path.

## From a law to a local result

The [Deerfield trial](experiments/evidence-trials/results.md#3-from-a-charging-grant-to-an-operating-site) traced a charging-infrastructure grant to a reported opening. Use a different recipient to test whether the method transfers. The Federal Highway Administration identifies section 11401 and 23 U.S.C. 151(f) as authority for its discretionary program. This supplies a source-backed legislative connection to the grant provision in the clustering pilot. Select a named award and a similarly named project in the same area as a control for the new case. Freeze the observation date before following the evidence. [FHWA program and statutory citation](https://highways.fhwa.dot.gov/iija/fact-sheets/charging-and-fueling-infrastructure-discretionary-grant-program) and [provision comparison](experiments/section-clusters.md).

1. Identify the program, recipient, award identifier, project and performance location from official records. Follow transactions by award identifier. Show obligated amounts, subsequent adjustments and any available outlays with their reporting dates. The recipient's address and the place of performance can differ. [Treasury's spending guide](https://www.usaspending.gov/federal-spending-guide).
2. Seek a recipient or agency completion record tying the award to a specific site. Check address, station identifier, operating status and date against a dated station inventory. The inventory's opening date can come from its first appearance in a network feed. A nearby station or a similarly named recipient alone leaves the award-to-site link unresolved. [Station data definitions](https://afdc.energy.gov/data_download/alt_fuel_stations_format).
3. Report verified and unresolved links separately, along with the reporting lag at each stage. An operating site answers a delivery question. To assess claims about improved access, specify a population, a before-and-after measure and a credible comparison before estimating an effect.
