Kelly, with Schiff and Slotkin, announces bill to shut down the DOJ "Anti-Weaponization Fund" and calls it corruption

Sen. Mark Kelly, with Sens. Adam Schiff and Elissa Slotkin, held a press conference to announce the Drain the Slush Fund Act. According to the article, the bill would shut down the Justice Department's "Anti-Weaponization Fund" and stop taxpayer money from going to the President or his allies.

Kelly calls the fund a "$1.8 billion slush fund" and "corruption in broad daylight." He says the bill is meant to stop any president, Democrat or Republican, from making such deals. The bill has been introduced. It has not passed.
