Design prototype, 6 November 2026

Civic Lens redesign prototype

Seventeen pages of the new public site, built from real records. Check the look and the reader flow before step 2 code starts. Each page works at phone and desktop width, in light and dark.

Theme exploration

Each page has a theme switcher at the right of the prototype bar: Current, Poster, Newsroom and Mosaic. The words and data do not change. Only the look changes. Your choice stays as you move between pages. Start with Home, Vote and Official. The 5 core pages (home, home after ZIP, official, vote, bill) are fully themed. The other pages take the theme colors and fonts, but some spacing stays as before.

What is real and what is EXAMPLE

  • Real: the vote kinds and casts on the vote rows, the Pressley casts and vote titles in the statement rows, the live finding that Pressley has no published statements, the 542 officials count, and the bill status time (5 Nov 2026, 09:57 UTC).
  • Statements: the live site holds statements (for example from Mark Kelly and Alex Padilla, collected 29 to 30 Oct 2026), but none from Pressley, Clark or about H.R. 5334. Every statement slot in this prototype is EXAMPLE. A real record for format: Kelly statement page. The live data often has no statement date, so the slots show "Date not stated. Source seen ..." once.
  • EXAMPLE: the "Records as of" time, the new, vacant and former member pages, ZIPs 42223, 20001 and 96910, all correction and withdrawal details, and the popover wording. Notices change only the top of the page. The counts below them stay as in revision 1.
