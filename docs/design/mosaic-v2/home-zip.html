Why there are no senators

Only states elect senators. A delegate speaks for the district or territory in the House, and can take part in committee work. A delegate has no vote on passage, so passage roll calls do not list this member's cast.

The roll calls we list for a delegate are the ones where the House Clerk record shows a cast. Read how delegates differ
