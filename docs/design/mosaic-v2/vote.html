The House agreed to Senate changes to H.R. 5334, the Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026. The motion passed.

AI summary. Drafted by an AI model from reviewed records.

The House voted on a motion to concur in amendments the Senate made to H.R. 5334. The motion passed, which means the House accepted the Senate's changes to the bill. The bill concerns sanctions on Russia and Iran, which are U.S. measures that restrict economic and other dealings with those countries.
