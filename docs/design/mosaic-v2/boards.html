Who counts, and what the numbers mean

Count: split votes where the member voted Yes or No with the other side's majority, against most Democrats. Base: split votes where the member cast Yes or No. Present and not voting are not counted.

Only members of the House Democrats with at least 25 split votes cast are listed. A shorter record could put a member first by chance. A vote against the party is not a mistake. A vote with the party is not a sign of good work.
