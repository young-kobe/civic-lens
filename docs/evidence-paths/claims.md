# Phase 1. Claims and evidence

## Two opening reader questions

**What did House roll call 325 decide?** In release 1416 the vote page identified suspension procedure, a two-thirds threshold, and the tally. The purpose of H.R. 9600 was to approve a location for a Thomas Paine memorial. Reps. Raskin and Spartz discussed the measure in a release on November 17. Join the applicable bill text, House decision, motion polarity, and dated statement. Attribute each quote to its exact speaker and host release. Distinguish House approval from enactment and a completed memorial. The external statement is a candidate source. The [published sample](experiments/published-graph.md) found no published relationship records. [Bill](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/BILLS-119hr9600rh), [roll call](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2026325?Page=2), and [sponsors' statement](https://raskin.house.gov/2026/11/raskin-spartz-legislation-finalizing-details-for-national-memorial-honoring-indispensable-american-patriot-thomas-paine-passes-house).

**Which veterans-benefits claims can a reader check?** The proclamation mentions a fiscal count, a decline, an executive action, and a housing goal. Its gratitude to veterans also supplies a useful example of a passage to skip. The two pilot executive records exposed metadata sources in our sample. Capture and validate the official body before extracting a publishable claim. Use R-61 for executive source coverage. [Proclamation](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-16/pdf/2026-23348.pdf) and [executive action pipeline](../architecture/executive-actions.md).

| Proposition | Evidence to seek | Public result it could justify |
|---|---|---|
| VA processed a record 3.1 million disability claims this year | Fiscal period, population, and comparable historical counts. VA reports 3,111,399 disability compensation claims in FY 2026. | Agency-reported count consistent with VA publications. Independent confirmation needs a separate source lineage (GAO or OIG). [VA report](https://news.va.gov/press-room/va-in-fy-2026-processes-the-most-claims-ever/). |
| The backlog declined by more than 70% during the term | One consistent weekly series for both endpoints, 12-month pre-period trend, inflow/completion flow context, and exact cells. | Tier 1 *measured change* with endpoint sensitivity range and no causal claim. | A September order accelerated benefits access | Separate the signature and directed tasks from completion and service outcomes. | The order documents the directive. Track the 120-day contract task and look for subsequent evidence of completion and timeliness. [Executive order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/09/accelerating-access-to-veterans-benefits-and-employment-opportunities/). |
| The administration aims to house up to 6,000 homeless veterans by 2028 | Preserve the qualifier, target population, due date, and unit. Look for progress reports. | A commitment with a deadline. People housed, available beds, and appropriated funds measure different things. |

The House passed the Major Richard Star Act on November 12. The proclamation was signed on November 10. The vote fits the issue timeline. Its existence cannot verify an outcome that the president described as already achieved.

## Proposed workflow and ownership

1. The collector fetches bounded official material under existing source rules. Separate campaign remarks, official actions, agency measurements, office narratives, and quoted third parties by origin. Never code-approve a quote from another office's release or a quoted third party as the host official's position.
2. Application proposes atomic claims from the saved body. Classify factual actions, numbers or comparisons, outcome or causal claims, positions, and future commitments. Preserve the original passage, exact speaker, host release, qualifiers, units, population, and time window. Split a statement that claims both an action and its effect.
3. Asynchronous candidate retrieval runs in a bounded worker stage (never inside synchronous review-lock transactions) using explicit identifiers, official names, dates, and program text in order of trust. Seek contrary evidence and independent source families. An LLM may propose questions, candidates, and devil's-advocate rubric challenges. Code validates spans, XML casts, edition dates, and defined quantities. A missing body, ambiguous speaker, or missing baseline becomes a visible gap.
4. Use the existing review queue and exact-version decision pattern under the solo-maintainer review budget. The maintainer decides disputed meaning, attribution, and outcome framing. Publication includes only eligible reviewed revisions and cited evidence. Withdrawals follow `WithdrawalCascade` on active releases plus the release-independent withdrawal tombstone ledger so rollbacks cannot resurrect withdrawn records. The public API exposes published contracts. [Relationship drafting and review](../architecture/statements.md#relationship-drafting), [analysis](../architecture/analysis.md), and [publication](../architecture/publication.md).

## Proposed relational design

Under accepted decisions [D-19 and D-20](../evidence-paths.md#pending-decisions), the private pilot extends the existing `RelationshipRevision` and `ReviewReasons` owners first instead of introducing a parallel review subsystem:

- Add optional `TargetPassageSpan` (precedent: `ActionTextCitation`), required `TargetRole` (`SpecificMotion`, `WholeBillPackage`, `Procedural`, `NoIndividualCast`, `BillEditionSponsorship`), and `MatchBasis` (`ExplicitBillNumber`, `OfficialCitation`, `LexicalMatch`, `SemanticMatch`) to `RelationshipRevision` for private pilot use.
- Add `ReviewReasonCode.InsufficientEvidence` on `Reject`, with conditional reopen in `RelationshipStaleness` when the input fingerprint or `as_of_utc` changes, and store `candidate_set_digest`, `retrieval_method_version`, and `as_of_utc` on the decision context.
- Represent a statement touching both a bill provision and a roll-call vote as two typed edges sharing one `StatementQuote` span (`Statement -> Action` with passage span and `Statement -> Vote` with role and motion polarity).
- Bind sponsorships with `SponsoredOn` against bill edition dates and flag full substitute replacements ("shell bills").

Only after the private pilot passes and multi-source outcome audits (such as VA workbooks or USAspending awards) enter production scope should the owner consider a composite assessment subsystem with these responsibilities:

| Unit | Identity and meaning |
|---|---|
| Claim and claim revision | Stable claim identity and an immutable revision. Bind the source record kind, record revision, exact extraction, and span. Store the normalized proposition separately from the quote. Structured values can be unknown. |
| Evidence link | A directed relation between a claim revision and a typed evidence version. Cite a span, official fact, or dataset cell with its source digest. Keep the source family, event date, observation date, and evidence availability date when known. |
| Assessment revision | Bind one claim revision and the exact selected links. Record question, as-of cutoff, method version, calculation, contrary evidence, reviewer, and limitations. A new finding adds a revision. |
| Published insight | Select the eligible assessment and evidence revisions in one release. Approval requires current citations, publication checks them against the release, `WithdrawalCascade` refuses withdrawal of a cited record while a finding cites it, and the monotonic withdrawal tombstone ledger prevents rollbacks from resurrecting withdrawn findings. |

Queue work with its trigger in the same transaction for lightweight subject enqueueing, while keeping multi-bill semantic candidate search in an asynchronous worker stage. Replaying the same extraction and task version preserves identity. Exercise duplicate imports, stale review, withdrawal, rollback, and failed runs that retry. The approval of an exact quote establishes what was said. An assessment of its accuracy needs its own evidence.

The outcome wording follows the three tiers in [pipeline rules](pipeline.md#rules-for-every-connection): *measured change* (default, with no causal claim and a 12-month pre-trend), *attributed cause* (quoted speaker only), and *independent evaluation* (GAO, OIG, or peer-reviewed study). The first assessment labels to test in the pilot are supported for the specified scope, conflicts with the specified evidence, mixed, insufficient evidence, and target pending. [AVeriTeC study](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/cd86a30526cd1aff61d6f89f107634e4-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html).

## How to add an issue

The tracked issue is a way to find evidence across sources. Use a second, finer unit for comparison: a policy mechanism affecting a defined population. For example, cap patient cost sharing for Medicare insulin and reduce insulin list prices for all buyers are different propositions. A spending bill can change health, energy, and taxes in separate sections. A single provision can have several issue labels.

1. **Configure the issue.** Reuse `config/issues.json` with its stable ID, definition, mapped policy areas, and inclusion and exclusion examples. Add examples of neighboring issues that are easy to confuse. Allow an empty label set. Treat repeated unmapped passages as candidates for a new curated issue. [Catalog](../../config/issues.json) and [existing bill issue selection](../../src/CivicLens.Application/Issues/BillIssueChoice.cs).
2. **Find eligible passages.** Segment the bill edition into provisions, preserving subsection boundaries and exact spans. Save the claim span from the statement or order separately. Use official identifiers first, then lexical and semantic retrieval across issues. A bill's primary policy area is a useful hint, though a secondary provision of an omnibus bill can fall outside it today. Measure those misses under R-54 and the policy-area gate baseline before changing the gate.
3. **Propose a relationship.** An AI classifier can propose zero or more issue IDs for each passage, plus the actor, action, target population, mechanism, amount, and period where stated. Keep the model run and exact citations. Compare each proposed pair at that level. A broad policy goal can match a subject while leaving the speaker's view of the specific provision unknown.
4. **Attach the official action with role, polarity, and edition.** Traverse from the member's dated cast to the roll-call question, its `ProceduralEffect` / `MotionPolarity`, and its target bill, amendment, or motion. Bind the bill version before the chamber and the status of the provision at that point. For sponsorships, bind `SponsoredOn` to the edition current on that date and flag full substitute replacement. An amendment directly deciding one provision supplies more specific evidence than an omnibus final vote. A motion to table, recommit, invoke cloture, or waive a budget rule can invert Yea/Nay meaning and must state what Yea operationally did. Record absence, voice vote, unanimous consent, and missing text explicitly.
5. **Apply the required public wording.** Distinguish *discussed the same issue*, *addressed the same proposal*, *voted on the whole bill*, *voted on the specific motion*, *voted on a procedural step*, and *later outcome observed*. This ladder is a mandatory pilot labeling rule, not a deferred choice. A package or procedural vote offered as provision evidence counts as an unsupported attribution. Whenever a provision quote sits near a package vote, state the bill's full scope and show any direct amendment vote beside or above it.

Compare three methods on the same independently labeled spans against a realistic full-release distractor corpus: the existing policy-area candidate gate, lexical or embedding retrieval over all configured issues, and a multi-label model reading the definition and provision together. Score missed secondary issues, false topical links, wrong speakers, and unsupported attributions separately. Keep a change in the catalog version distinct from a change in model version.

The [cross-issue replay](experiments/issue-mapping.md) and the other eight trial bill families are development data. For the pilot holdout, draw unseen bill families using the seeded deterministic script in [pipeline evaluation](pipeline.md#evaluation), including single-issue bills, omnibus provisions, competing bills on the same issue, amended text, shell-bill substitutes, voice votes, and untracked topics. Test the same fields on an official statement, an executive order, and a multi-speaker office release. An office or party affiliation defines a cohort; it never supplies a position for members who have not spoken.

## From a law to a local result

The [Deerfield trial](experiments/evidence-trials/results.md#3-from-a-charging-grant-to-an-operating-site) traced a charging-infrastructure grant to a reported opening. When a delivery stage enters scope, test transfer on 5 different recipients including at least 2 similarly named decoys in the same area. Freeze the observation date before following the evidence. [FHWA program and statutory citation](https://highways.fhwa.dot.gov/iija/fact-sheets/charging-and-fueling-infrastructure-discretionary-grant-program) and [provision comparison](experiments/section-clusters.md).

1. Identify the program, recipient, award identifier (FAIN), project, and performance location from official records. Follow transactions by FAIN. Show obligated amounts, subsequent adjustments, and any available outlays with their reporting dates. The recipient's address and the place of performance can differ. [Treasury's spending guide](https://www.usaspending.gov/federal-spending-guide).
2. Seek a recipient or agency completion record tying the award to a specific site. Check address, station identifier, operating status, and date against a dated station inventory. A nearby station or similarly named recipient alone leaves the award-to-site link unresolved. [Station data definitions](https://afdc.energy.gov/data_download/alt_fuel_stations_format).
3. Report verified and unresolved links separately, along with the reporting lag at each stage. Never assign local delivery credit to a named official without an explicit reviewed link. To assess claims about improved access, specify a population, a before-and-after measure, and a credible comparison before estimating an effect.
